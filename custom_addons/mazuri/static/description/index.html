Mazuri

Connect Mazuri for Cloud Agents and enhanced workspace context — sync your FMCG catalog, receive retailer orders, and track Pay-in-30 financing.

One-click connect

Authorize Mazuri from your browser, GitHub-App style. No manual API keys or OAuth setup — install the app and click Connect Odoo in Mazuri.

Catalog & inventory sync

Mazuri reads your products and stock levels so retailers can search and order your catalog in real time — with live availability.

Sales orders

Retailer orders placed on Mazuri land directly in Odoo Sales as purchase-ready orders with line items and retailer details.

Embedded financing

Track Mazuri-financed retailer orders (Pay in 30, interest free) with disbursement, due date, and repayment status — plus an automatic "Mazuri Pay in 30" payment term.

The app is free to install. A Mazuri workspace is required to activate sync and financing — visit mazuri.app to get started.
